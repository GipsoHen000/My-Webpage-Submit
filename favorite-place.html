My Favorite Vacation Spot:

Great Wolf Lodge

Not really a singular location, but very similar across all the locations I've seen. It's a mix of a hotel and an indoor waterpark, along with an arcade and different varing experiences depending on the location. Such as glow-in-the-dark mini-golf, an animatronic show, and MagiQuest, a, as far as I can tell, exclusive experience where you get a wand and are able to interact with different physical locations and props to earn xp, or something. I never really got into it, but it is very cool! I've been there a total of 3 times, each for a birthday! 2 of them were mine, and inbetween, one for my little sister.
