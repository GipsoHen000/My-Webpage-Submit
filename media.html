DELTARUNE
-------------------

An RPG-style game where you (seemingly) play as Kris, the only human in a town of friendly monsters. One day in school, you can the class bully, Susie, have to get chalk in the storage closet down the hall, causing them to fall into a pit of dakrness. They awake in a vibrant "Dark World", guided by a character named Ralsei, who informs them they are the 3 heros of the prophecy. Along the way they learn more about themselves, bond, and band together to defeat the mysterious Roaring Knight, and seal each Dark Fountain to prevent the apocolypse from starting, meeting all sorts of colorful characters that resemble real world objects come to life!

The game includes thrilling gameplay, funny jokes, suprisingly heartfelt story, complex characters, mystery, and even a secret route to the game.

MINECRAFT
-------------------

In Minecraft, players explore a procedurally generated world with virtually infinite terrain made up of blocks. They can discover and extract raw materials, craft tools and items, build structures, fight hostile mobs, and cooperate with or compete against other players in multiplayer. In addition, the game's large community offers a wide variety of user-made content, such as modifications, servers, player skins, texture packs, and custom maps, which add new game mechanics and possibilities!
